Help & Support

Questions about a booking, your listing, or your account? Email us at hello@hivebuilt.dev and we’ll get back to you, usually within a day.

Travelers

  • When am I charged? Your card is held when you request a spot and charged only when the host accepts. If they decline, the hold is released.
  • Cancelling: open Trips and tap Cancel trip. Cancelling at least 24 hours before check-in is free. See the Cancellation Policy.
  • Arrival and departure: each spot shows when you can arrive and when you need to leave. Overnight-only spots need your vehicle gone by the departure time every morning.
  • Talking to your host: use Messages on your trip.

Hosts

  • Listing your space: tap List your lot in the app or on tryrvnb.com. We review each listing before it goes live.
  • Getting paid: set up payouts once from the Host tab. You’re paid automatically after each stay.
  • Requests: you approve every booking before anyone parks. You can block dates or pause your listing anytime.

Your account

  • Can’t log in? If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that button. Otherwise, use Forgot password on the login page.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, Profile → Delete account.
  • Your data: see the Privacy Policy.
